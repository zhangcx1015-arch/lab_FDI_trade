# U.S. Export Destinations: 2015 vs. 2025
One thing I noticed from the graph is that Canada and Mexico remained the two 
largest export destinations for the United States in both 2015 and 2025. Their 
export values also increased noticeably over time, which suggests that North
American trade relationships continue to play a major role in U.S. exports.

China was still one of the top export destinations in 2025, but the gap between 
China and the other two top countries became much larger. I also found it 
interesting that Taiwan and Switzerland appeared in the top 10 list in 2025, 
while Hong Kong and Belgium were in the top 10 in 2015 but not in 2025. I think 
this reflects changes in global supply chains and shifting trade patterns over 
time, especially as trade relationships and regional economic connections 
continue to change.
